# Protest design

The selected direction is an original street poster studio: oversized condensed typography, warm paper, ink, acid yellow, and vermilion. Inspired by Amplifier's poster hierarchy and Patagonia Action Works' emphasis on concrete action, plus WikiLeaks' source oriented publication and direct document links. No claims about traffic or conversion are made.

Tokens: paper #f3f0e8, ink #171713, lime #dcfa52, orange #ef4c33. Anton for display type, Barlow Condensed for UI headings, system sans for fields and reading. Rectangular buttons, 1px ink rules, no rounded card grid. Desktop workspace is 44% editor and 56% dark preview. Mobile stacks editor and preview with wrapping navigation.

Allowed primary copy: PROTEST.; The guide; Open project; Save project; MAKE SOME NOISE.; Turn a reason to gather into a flier and a website. Yours to make. Yours to publish.; Free. Open source. On your terms.; PURPOSE; DETAILS; DESIGN; PUBLISH; Start with your why.; One clear reason. One concrete request.; Protest title; Why are you gathering?; What change do you want?; Organizer; Continue to details; FLIER; WEBSITE; Fictional example. Make it yours.

Functional extensions: local draft status and new draft control; detail fields; three poster palettes; Letter/A4 PDF, square and story PNG; guide; GitHub sign in, publication review and status. These share the same visual system. Exported materials are generated from live text rather than a raster mockup. A blank new project never silently publishes the example.

The example is fictional. No real event is implied. Publishing requires an explicit confirmation of the exact prepared content and destination. Private organizer notes are excluded from public materials and repositories. Private project backups deliberately retain them.
